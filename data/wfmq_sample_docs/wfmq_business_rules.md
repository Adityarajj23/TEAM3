# Workforce Management Quality Platform — Statutory Business Rules & Payroll Policy

## 1. Statutory Time Tracking & Rounding Rules (BR-ATT)
- Time punch timestamps shall be rounded to the nearest quarter hour using the standard 7-minute rounding threshold.
- Shifts exceeding 6 consecutive hours require a minimum mandatory unpaid meal deduction of 30 minutes.
- The payroll calculation process should execute fast and efficiently without delay as needed.

## 2. Premium Overtime Compensation Rules (BR-PAY-CONTRADICTION)
- All overtime hours shall be paid at a multiplier of 2.0x base hourly wage for weekend and statutory holiday work shifts.
- Any manual override of calculated overtime premiums requires dual-authorization from both Finance and Operations department heads.

## 3. Paid Time Off & Absence Governance (BR-LVE-CONTRADICTION)
- Employees become eligible for annual paid leave after a probation period of 30 days under collective bargaining agreements.
- Unused annual leave balances up to a maximum of 40 hours may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year.
- The system shall prohibit negative leave balances under all standard employment agreements.
